About the Event

WinterHaus is a festive holiday market bringing together local makers, artists, food vendors, and winter traditions for a weekend of seasonal shopping and celebration.

Explore handmade gifts, enjoy seasonal treats, listen to live music, and gather with friends and family in a welcoming winter atmosphere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WinterHaus free to attend?

Yes. Admission to the market is free. Individual workshops or special activities may have separate fees.

Is the market family-friendly?

Yes. WinterHaus welcomes visitors of all ages and includes seasonal activities for families and children.

Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved pets are welcome in outdoor market areas. Service animals are welcome throughout the event.
